MediaForge: third-party software
================================

MediaForge is proprietary software, copyright C2 Software.

To do its work MediaForge starts the separate programs listed below. They are installed alongside the
application for convenience, and each remains under its own licence. They are independent programs:
MediaForge runs them and reads their output. It does not link to them or contain their code.

You may replace any of them with a copy of your own in MediaForge > Settings > Tools > Choose Location.
The licence texts referred to below are in the same folder as this file.

4. Interface components
   MediaForge's window is built with Tauri (MIT or Apache-2.0), React (MIT) and Lucide icons (ISC),
   which are compiled into the app, and uses Microsoft Edge WebView2, which is part of Windows.
   Pictures are read and written with the image-rs libraries (image, image-webp, png, gif, tiff) and
   zune-jpeg, all available under the MIT licence and also compiled in: https://github.com/image-rs
   and https://github.com/etemesi254/zune-image.
